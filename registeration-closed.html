About Momentum’26

Momentum began as a radical idea. Two years ago, 400+ visionaries from academia and industry convened around a shared conviction, that the long-standing compact between them was ready to be reimagined.

By 2025, the idea had become a reckoning. More voices joined. Global uncertainty was no longer a subject of considered debate. It had become the very atmosphere in which institutions and organizations functioned.

Now in 2026, the reckoning has a shape.

The new era of talent has dawned. Companies are increasingly prioritizing AI-ready talent, and the opportunities for those prepared for this shift have never been bigger.

The programmer who can direct AI is the most sought-after engineer in the room. The analyst who can interpret AI output is the one driving strategy. AI is here to augment human potential, and institutions that prepare students for that reality are the ones that will define the next decade of talent.

A degree still opens the door. But in a world where AI can code, what wins the room is no longer the syntax. It is the purpose with which intelligence is wielded.

Momentum’26 convenes at the center of this transformation, a one-day symposium with a provocation as its compass: Beyond degree. Beyond stream. Beyond code.

This is the inspiration behind Momentum’26, our high-stakes symposium with the country’s most consequential minds.

One day, one room, and one playbook ready to be written: on how India learns, hires, and redefines employability in the age of AI.

Topics for Panel Discussions

New roles, new frontiers: Inside India's specialization revolution
  • The expanding job map: New sectors, leaner role clusters, and the end of generalist pyramids
  • AI as job architect: Redesigning role boundaries, commoditizing routine, creating roles that did not exist
  • Who owns the transition: Closing the gap between domain-tech fluency and what institutions currently create
Re-imagining talent and skills evaluation for an AI-enabled workforce
  • The evolution of assessments from college hiring to continuous capability evaluation
  • Balancing technology, trust, and human judgment in talent decisions
  • The institutional imperative of preparing students for a world of ongoing assessment
Purpose-driven careers: When organizational purpose meets individual ambition
  • The reversal of information asymmetry between the employer and candidate
  • The emerging drivers of employer brand preference among top talent
  • The strategic imperative of building employer narratives before placement season
Across the Table: Three Perspectives, One Conversation
  • Campus hiring in transition: How AI, business priorities, and talent expectations are redefining entry-level hiring.
  • Bridging readiness and reality: Aligning employer expectations, academic preparedness, and student aspirations in an AI-first world.
  • Preparing tomorrow's talent: The future of campus hiring, emerging skills, and the evolving role of academia and industry.

HackVega 2.0 Hackathon Finale

HackVega 2.0 is not a coding competition. It is a branch-neutral, AI-integrated problem-solving challenge run across India's top engineering colleges, identifying judgment, adaptability, and real-world capability along the way. Open to the 2026, 2027, 2028, and 2029 batches, the Momentum’26 finale crowns the winners, and activates access to India's most rigorously pre-assessed graduate talent pool.
