The Symposium

The Reckoning
Has A Shape.

Momentum began as a radical idea. Two years ago, 400+ visionaries from academia and industry convened around a shared conviction, that the long-standing compact between them was ready to be reimagined.

In an age of AI, the boundaries that once defined who could learn what, and who could work where, are dissolving.

IT intake stands at a two-decade nadir. Yet AI-linked hiring has surged 41% in BFSI, 38% in Healthcare, 31% in Retail, and 30% in Logistics. The opportunities have not contracted; they have migrated.

What emerges is an invitation:

  • Reimagine learning paradigms
  • Redefine core employability
  • Forge worthy academia–industry partnerships
